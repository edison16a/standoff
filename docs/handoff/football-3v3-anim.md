# Football 3v3: animation presets, handoff

A running note between the four steps of the Football 3v3 animation work. The owner's request is in the workflow's `owner-request.md`: NBA 2K and Madden feel, authored presets picked by the game logic before a move starts, blended with the physics underneath, never floating.

## Step 1: controls, control switch, throw meter, QB facing (done)

* **Keyboard player** (`src/games/football-3v3/keyboard/`). `binding.ts` is the card and the binding, `controller.ts` maps keys to what the phone sends right now (from the host's phone state), `throw-keys.ts` holds the throw meter on Space or the left mouse button and aims with the arrows or the mouse. Tests in `binding.test.ts`.
* **QB faces his target** (`engine/passer-facing.ts`, `engine/motion.ts`). Root cause: `turn()` faced the run whenever the stick was let go, so a QB dropping back (bots always, people after the release) turned his back to the field and threw with his chest to his own end zone. Now a passer backpedals facing downfield, snaps square to the receiver through the throw at `PASS.turnRate`, and follows through along the throw. The ball leaves the hand toward the target (`handSpot`), not along the facing, so the flight never depends on the body. Tests in `engine/passer-facing.test.ts` (person QB backpedalling, a swing pass behind him, every bot throw in a game).
* **One player control switch** (`engine/control.ts`). `Athlete.pilot` is the phone steering an athlete now; `seat` stays the owner (name, stats, results). On a pass or pitch release to a computer teammate the passer's phone takes him over (`handOver`), the computer plays the passer, and every phone gets its own player back when a play lines up (`restoreControl` in `phases.ts` `enter`). Views carry the pilot as `seat`, so the ring, the tag, the phone pad, buzzes and replay votes follow. The ring flies to the new player (`render/figures/control-switch.ts`). Tests in `engine/control.test.ts`.
* **Throw meter** (`engine/pass-meter.ts` pure grading and quality, `engine/meter-live.ts` the running meter and bot timing). Ping pong bar, green by arm, gold fixed and tiny. The grade sets `PassQuality` (line, wobble, hang time, drop, pick, read), carried on the throw action and `PassInfo.quality`, applied in `throw-error.ts`, `aim.ts` (`leadPass` time scale), `catching.ts` (read radius) and `catch/touch.ts` (hold and pick odds). Phone: `phone/components/ThrowMeter.tsx`, protocol `hold` and `throw.heldMs`, state `throwWindow`. Big screen: `render/figures/throw-meter-sprite.ts`, hidden in the showcase film (no tags there). Tests in `engine/pass-meter.test.ts`.
* **Trailer**: the seeded showcase game moved with these changes. `showcase/trailer.ts` shots were only retimed so `trailer.test.ts` passes; the hit shot now shows the Blaze receiver (id 8) catching as the Storm's corner (id 2) dives at him, a placeholder. The media files were not filmed again.

## Step 2: tackles, misses and running weight (done)

* **Selection in the engine** (`engine/tackle-preset.ts`). `approachFor` picks the approach as the lunge starts, from the angle to the carrier's run, the gap and the two men's masses: `drive` head on and close for a man his size, `ankle` for a smaller man head on or a much lighter one from the side, `shoestring` from behind when the carrier pulls away, `wrap` otherwise. It rides on the lunge action (`approach`) and in the view (`lunge`). At contact the momentum still decides (`hit.ts`); if he goes down `finishFor` makes it `gang` when `helpersFor` finds a defender within `PICK.helpRange`, turns a drive that did not move him back into a wrap, and otherwise keeps the approach. Lineman grabs trip the carrier as a shoestring with no bound tackler. Tests in `engine/tackle-preset.test.ts`.
* **Binding** (`engine/tackle-bind.ts`, numbers in `engine/tackle-moves.ts`). `bindTackle` puts carrier, tackler and helpers down with a `TackleBind` on the down action (kind, role carrier, tackler or pile, partner, the tackle's line `f`, the side the man came from, and where he met the carrier). The carrier slides by the preset's `slide` (drag, plus a push while a drive's legs churn, `motion.ts` `slideDown`). `followBinds` runs every sub step in `bodies.ts` and holds the tackler and the pile at keyed places on the carrier through the fall, the roll and their get up (stepping clear as they rise); `collide.ts` skips men of the same tackle. Down times are all under the 2.2 s whistle break. Tests in `engine/tackle-bind.test.ts`.
* **Poses** (`render/anim/tackle/`). `carrier.ts`, `tackler.ts` (and the pile) hold one keyed track per preset in seconds from the hit, authored for a tackler on the carrier's left; `index.ts` mirrors for the right (the carrier keeps the ball arm) and plays the track with `getup.ts`, which gets each man up by how he lies (front, back, rolled, side, seat). `lunges.ts` draws the lunge per approach. `misses.ts` has whiff (belly flop and log roll), missed (dodged, shoulder landing, tumble), shed (run through, spun onto his seat) and juked (ankles broken, sits down). `stumble.ts` is the lurch added on top of a fooled man still on his feet. The pose has a new `barrel` joint, a roll about the spine applied before the tilt, which `approach` eases the short way round. `choose.ts` routes lunges, downs and stumbles here. Tests in `render/anim/tackle/tackle.test.ts`.
* **Juke beats** (`engine/juke-beat.ts`). When a carrier's dodge window opens, heavy defenders (linemen or over 97 kg) close in front stumble (`Athlete.stumble`, plus stagger) or, inside 1.05 m with agility under 6, fall (`down` cause `juked`). The lurch goes the way the man bit. Linemen now run `updateDown` in `world.ts` and a downed lineman does not grab. Tests in `engine/juke-beat.test.ts`.
* **Running weight.** Engine: grip scales with `(refMass / mass) ^ MOVE.gripMass` and only `MOVE.carve` of it is left for turning at top speed (`motion.ts`). Drawing: `gait.ts` `heft` rocks the hips over the planted foot and sinks its knee, more for heavy builds; `choose.ts` `plantFor` plants the outside foot when braking into a cut; locked linemen carry their pair's lurch as `ax` and lean with it in the block pose. Tests in `engine/motion.test.ts`.
* **Lab** (`showcase/lab-stage.ts`, `showcase/lab-scenes.ts`). Staged moments with the real engine pieces and a forced outcome: wrap, drive, ankle, shoestring, gang, whiff, missed, shed, juked, stumble, cut, heavy. `?lab=<move>&labstep=1` exposes `window.__fbLabStep(seconds, draw)` so a review script steps every frame. A contact sheet script that worked well: step at 1/60, draw at the chosen times, copy the canvas into a 2D sheet in the page and screenshot that.
* **Showcase.** The presets moved the seeded game again; `SHOWCASE_SEED` is now 347 (long first touchdown with a side step, then a catch over the middle wrapped up by the corner at 55.6 s, and a drive tackle at 62.5 s). `trailer.ts` and `film-cams.ts` (CATCHER 2, JUKER 1, TACKLER 7) are timed to it and the hit shot now shows a real wrap. The media files were not filmed again.

## Left for later steps

* **Step 3, throws, catches, interceptions, blockers.** The throw keeps one keyed motion (`render/anim/throwing.ts`, release at `PASS.windup`). The QB always faces the target in the engine, so presets can assume yaw points at the receiver during the `throw` action. `PassInfo.quality.grade` is there to pick a throw preset (a weak floater, a hot fastball) and catch or pick presets (a perfect ball is never read by a defender: `read` is 0). Catch is still `catchPose` reaching in the gait. Blockers are `blockPose` with the new lurch lean; a pancake could reuse the juked fall and `barrel`. The pattern from step 2 works well: pick in the engine, carry the pick on the action and in the view, play keyed tracks in seconds on the action clock, mirror by side, lab stage with a forced outcome.

The throw meter sprite is off in the showcase by design.
