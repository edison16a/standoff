# Zombie Survival gun sound credits

Every recording here comes from Freesound and is released under Creative Commons 0 (CC0 1.0, public domain dedication, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). No credit is required, but the authors asked for it, so here it is. The licence of each source was checked on its own Freesound page on 2026-10-12.

The files are short cuts from the sources' high quality previews: trimmed to one event, made mono, high passed at 30 Hz, low passed at 16 kHz, peak normalised, faded at the end and encoded as MP3. Nothing else was changed. The game layers its own synthesis and a street echo on top (see `src/games/zombie-survival/audio/guns/`).

Some foley sources (marked airsoft) were recorded by their authors with full metal airsoft replicas, which share the real guns' parts and sound.
