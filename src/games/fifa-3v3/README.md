# FIFA 3v3

Status: in development.

## The idea

Three on three football for up to six players, with a computer goalkeeper in each goal and computer players filling any empty spots. Very good 3D graphics, lots of sound, animation and confetti. Simple and very fun with lots of friends.

* **Roster:** star characters modelled after famous players (Messi, Ronaldo and the like), each with stats that match their game. Each phone picks its own character.
* **Teams:** the person at the computer puts players on teams and starts the match.
* **Controls (phone held sideways like a controller):** a thumb stick on the left moves your player relative to the screen, dribbling on its own. Two buttons on the right: Shoot and Slide Tackle.
* **Shooting:** aims itself at the goal. The outcome is chance based and plays out with real looking ball physics: saves by the computer keeper, goals, shots off the post or the bar, shots over the bar.
* **Sound:** an announcer calling goals, the crowd, the ball, whistles, and a goal celebration with confetti.

Uses the phone gamepad in `src/games/kit/pad`. Rooms have six seats.
