# NBA 3v3

Status: in development.

## The idea

Three on three basketball on one half court for up to six players, with computer players filling any empty spots. Very good 3D graphics, lots of sound, animation and confetti.

* **Roster:** ten star characters modelled after famous players (Curry, LeBron and the like), each with stats that match their game: speed, shooting and strength. Each phone picks its own character, so no two players share one.
* **Teams:** the person at the computer puts players on teams and starts the game. Empty spots are computer players.
* **Controls (phone held sideways like a controller):** a thumb stick on the left moves your player relative to the screen, dribbling on its own. On the right, Shoot, Pass (to the nearest teammate) and Block. Block jumps to contest a shot or block a layup, and turns into Steal on defence: close enough to the ball carrier, it knocks the ball loose.
* **Shooting:** hold Shoot and release on a timing bar, like NBA 2K. Green means it goes in. Make it fairly easy to green. A contest lowers the odds. Misses and makes play out with real looking ball physics: swishes, bank shots, balls rolling round the rim and in, bouncing off the rim, off the backboard.
* **Driving:** a strong player running at the rim in line with it dunks, with a dunk animation for each character. Strength wins layups and dunks against weaker defenders.
* **Score:** first to 11. A scoreboard at the top with the score and a shot clock.
* **Sound:** an announcer calling "Dunk!" and "Three!", the crowd going "ooh" and cheering, sneakers squeaking, the ball bouncing, the net swishing, the shot clock horn.

Uses the phone gamepad in `src/games/kit/pad`. Rooms have six seats.
