# Boxing

Status: in development.

## The idea

A 3D boxing match played with your body in front of the computer's camera. No phones: a pose model runs in the browser on the player's own computer and reads where each boxer's head, shoulders, elbows and wrists are.

* **Players:** 1 against the computer, or 2 side by side in front of one camera. The player on the left of the camera image is player one.
* **Screen:** split screen, one view per boxer from behind their own shoulder. The boxers close the distance on their own.
* **Moves:** punch (left and right, jab and hook), block by holding both gloves up in front of the face, duck and dodge by dipping or leaning the head. A clean block opens a short window for a fast counter jab.
* **Health:** each boxer has a health bar. A knockdown, a count, and a winner with confetti.
* **Calibration:** stand in the middle of the frame, then show your guard, so the game learns your height, reach and guard.

## Look

Its own style: a proper ring under bright lights, detailed 3D boxers and gloves, sweat and impact sparks, a crowd.
