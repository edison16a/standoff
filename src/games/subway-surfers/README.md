# Subway Surfers

Status: in development.

## The idea

An endless runner on the rails, played with your whole body in front of the computer's camera. A pose model runs in the browser on the player's own computer.

* **Players:** 1, or 2 side by side in split screen (left of the camera image is player one).
* **Moves:** step left or right to change lanes, jump to jump, duck to roll under barriers.
* **Calibration:** stand in the middle of your half of the frame so the game learns where centre is and how tall you stand.
* **Power ups:** jump boots, a hoverboard that saves you from one crash, a coin magnet, a score multiplier.
* **Endless:** it speeds up over time. Coins and distance make the score, and the best scores stay on this computer.

## Look

Bright and colourful 3D: trains, tunnels, signals, graffiti, coins.
